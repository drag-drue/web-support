How to process a New Mzigo

Follow the steps below to create a new Mzigo. Before you begin, ensure you have the sender's details, receiver's details, package information, and payment details ready.

Step 1: Press Mzigo Button

From the dashboard, click the New Mzigo button to open the mzigo processing form.

Step 2: Enter Sender Details

Fill in all the required sender information, including the sender's name and phone number.
